Privacy Rights for Hatchlings & Heroes

This page explains how a player, parent, or guardian can exercise privacy rights relating to Hatchlings & Heroes ("the Game"). For details about the information involved, why it is used, and when another company may process it, read the **Hatchlings & Heroes Privacy Policy **

Effective date:** 30 -08- 2026
Last updated:** 30 -08- 2026

The Game uses local player profiles rather than registered online Hatchlings & Heroes accounts. Most gameplay information stays on the player's device, so most rights can be exercised directly on that device without sending information about a child to us.

01. Access and checking information

Local Profile Information

A parent or guardian can review most local information through the Game's screens.

Local profile information includes:

  • Nickname
  • Selected icon and title
  • Birth year
  • Timestamps
  • Gameplay progress associated with that profile

Backup Save & Data Export

A parent can also create a machine-readable copy through:

Settings > Family & Safety > Backup Save

The exported JSON backup may include:

  • Nicknames
  • Birth years
  • Scanned barcode values
  • Hatchlings
  • Gameplay progress
  • Inventory
  • Virtual currency
  • Achievements
  • Quests
  • Game settings
  • Other related local data

Important: Backup Security

Exported backups are not encrypted by the Game.

Store exported backup files privately and do not send them to us unless we specifically request a limited file for support and the parent agrees.

02. Correction of Profile Information

Profile nicknames, icons, and titles can be corrected through Manage Profiles.

Most gameplay information represents the player's actual Game activity and should not ordinarily be manually altered.

The current version does not provide an in-place birth-year editor. If the saved birth year is incorrect, a parent can delete and recreate that local profile.

Important: Profile deletion is permanent and removes the profile-scoped progress from the device.

An exported backup of the old profile will still contain the old birth year and should be deleted if it is no longer needed.

03. Deletion and Local-Profile Deregistration

Hatchlings & Heroes does not create a registered online player account. Its profiles are local records, so "account deletion" means deleting the local profile and its profile-scoped Game data.

To Delete a Profile

  1. Open the profile-selection area.
  2. Select Manage Profiles.
  3. Tap the delete control for the relevant profile.
  4. Confirm the permanent-deletion warning.
  5. Complete the parent gate.

Removing All Remaining Local Game Data

To remove all remaining local Game data, delete all profiles and then clear the app's storage in the device settings or uninstall the Game.

What Deletion Does Not Remove

Deletion from the Game does not remove:

  • Backup files previously exported to device storage, email, cloud storage, or another application.
  • Purchase records retained independently by Huawei, RevenueCat, Google, Apple, or another store/provider.
  • Information voluntarily sent in an email or support request where continued retention is legally permitted or required.

Those copies must be deleted through the destination or provider that holds them.

04. Withdrawing Consent and Permissions

Camera

Camera access can be withdrawn in the device's application-permission settings. The Game will stop receiving the live camera feed.

Barcode scanning will no longer work, but unrelated Game features remain available.

Previously decoded barcode values remain in local save data until the relevant profile or data is deleted.

Notifications

Turn off Care Reminders in Game Settings to cancel scheduled local reminders.

Notification permission can also be withdrawn in device settings. Other Game features remain available.

Backup Sharing

Backup export is parent-initiated. A parent can cancel before choosing a destination.

After a backup is shared, deletion or withdrawal must be handled through the receiving application or storage provider.

Purchases

Purchase processing occurs only when a parent passes the parent gate and initiates or restores a purchase.

Purchase cancellation, refund, and store-data controls are managed by the applicable storefront.

Transaction records may still be retained where necessary for fraud prevention, delivery integrity, accounting, or legal obligations.

Important: Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was already lawful before withdrawal.

05. Objecting to or Restricting Processing

Depending on the country, a parent or guardian may ask us to restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Because ordinary gameplay information remains locally on the device and is not available on our server, the fastest way to restrict it is normally to stop using the relevant feature, withdraw its permission, delete the local profile, clear app storage, or uninstall the Game.

Ways to Restrict Local Gameplay Data

  • Stop using the relevant Game feature.
  • Withdraw the relevant device permission.
  • Delete the local profile.
  • Clear the Game's app storage.
  • Uninstall the Game.

No Behavioural Advertising

The Game does not use personal information for behavioral advertising, targeted marketing, or advertising analytics.

As a result, there is no advertising-personalization setting within the Game.

06. Purchase-provider information

We cannot directly access or delete all information held independently by an app store or purchase provider. Use the provider's privacy tools or contact the provider for store-account, payment, order, or provider-side transaction information:

  • Huawei AppGallery privacy statement
  • HMS Core privacy information
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy , where Google Play applies
  • Apple App Store & Privacy , where Apple applies

7. Contact

To ask a privacy question or exercise a right relating to information being controled, a parent or guardian can contact:


Developer: Hatchlings & Heroes developer - Annuscha Botes
Privacy email: Send Email
Country/region: South-Africa


Use the email subject Hatchlings & Heroes Privacy. Please do not send unnecessary information about a child. Describe the request and the relevant storefront/device, but do not send passwords, full payment-card details, government identifiers, or sensitive QR/barcode contents.


Because I do not operate an online Hatchlings & Heroes account database, I may be unable to identify or retrieve local device data remotely. If a request concerns information voluntarily emailed to me or purchase-delivery information I control, I may request reasonable evidence that the requester is the player or the child's parent/legal guardian before acting.


I will respond within the period required by applicable law. If a request concerns data held by a store or purchase provider, I may direct the requester to that provider. A requester may also have the right to complain to the relevant local data-protection authority.

Privacy Policy for Hatchlings & Heroes​

✦

About This Privacy Policy

Hatchlings & Heroes (the "Game") is a child-friendly mobile game developed and published by me, the independent developer of Hatchlings & Heroes.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Game uses, where that information is stored, when it may be sent to another company or service provider, and the choices available to players, parents, and guardians.

This policy applies specifically to the Hatchlings & Heroes mobile application. The portfolio website that hosts this page may have its own separate privacy and cookie policy.

Your Choices

Instructions for accessing, correcting, copying, exporting, or deleting information, as well as withdrawing permissions, are provided on this page.


Effective date:** 30 -08- 2026
Last updated:** 30 -08- 2026
✦

Privacy Summary

This quick summary highlights the most important privacy points for parents and guardians. The detailed sections below explain each topic more fully.

  • The Game does not require an online Hatchlings & Heroes account.
  • Most profile and gameplay information stays on the player's device.
  • The Game has no public chat, public profiles, user-to-user trading, location sharing, behavioral advertising, or third-party advertising.
  • Camera access is used to read barcode values for the scanner feature. Camera images and video are not saved or uploaded by the Game.
  • Notifications are optional and are scheduled locally on the device.
  • Parents or guardians can export a local backup file and choose where to save or share it.
  • If real-money purchases are enabled, payment and transaction processing is handled by the applicable app store and, on supported storefronts, may also be handled by RevenueCat. I do not receive payment-card details.
  • The Game does not request location permission, access an advertising identifier such as Huawei OAID, or use personal information for advertising or marketing.

Want the Full Details?

This summary is provided for convenience and does not replace the full Privacy Policy. Please review the sections below for detailed information about local storage, permissions, purchases, backups, parental controls, privacy rights, security, and third-party services.

✦

1. Information Processed and Stored Locally

The Game creates local player profiles. Depending on the features used, the following information may be stored in the app's private storage on the device:

  • An in-game display name or nickname.
  • Birth year and whether the player is under 13, used to choose age-appropriate settings and protections.
  • An internal profile identifier and anonymous account identifier.
  • Selected profile icon, border, title, accessibility settings, sound settings, and reminder preferences.
  • Hatchlings, scanned barcode values, collection history, inventory, virtual currency, equipment, makeup, decorations, achievements, quests, battles, competitions, training results, NPC friendship, and other gameplay progress.
  • Profile creation and last-played timestamps.
  • The active profile identifier and parental-gate preference.
  • Purchase-delivery records such as transaction identifier, store/provider, product, virtual item amount, delivery status, and timestamps, where purchases are enabled.

Use a Nickname

Players should use a nickname rather than a real full name.

The Game does not ask for an email address, telephone number, home address, precise location, contacts, microphone recordings, or social-media account.

Local Save Data

Local gameplay save data is not sent to a Hatchlings & Heroes server because the Game currently has no developer-operated player-account or cloud-save service.

⌁

2. Camera and Barcode Scanner

Camera permission is requested only when the barcode scanner is used. The live camera feed is processed on the device so that the Game can read a barcode or code value and generate gameplay content.

Camera Data

The Game does not intentionally record, save, or upload camera photographs, video, or the live camera feed.

The decoded barcode value may be stored in local gameplay data and may therefore also appear in a parent-exported backup file.

What Players Should Scan

Players should scan ordinary product barcodes only.

Do not scan QR codes or other codes containing names, contact details, account credentials, payment information, or other personal information.

Camera Permission

Camera permission can be denied or withdrawn in the device settings.

The scanner will not work without camera permission, but other parts of the Game remain available.

♡

3. Birth Year, Children's Privacy, and Parental Controls

Hatchlings & Heroes is designed to provide a child-friendly experience. The Game uses a neutral birth-year screen to apply suitable settings.

The birth year is stored locally and is not intentionally transmitted to me.

Child-Friendly Features

The Game does not provide:

  • Public chat.
  • Public posting.
  • Public profiles.
  • Online player-to-player trading.
  • Location sharing.

The Game also does not display third-party advertisements or use behavioral advertising.

Parent-Gated Controls

Parent-gated controls protect sensitive actions such as:

  • Bank access.
  • Real-money purchase attempts.
  • Backup import and export.
  • Save reset.
  • Profile deletion.

Parent or Guardian Supervision

A parent or guardian should supervise profile creation, backup handling, and any purchase activity.

I do not knowingly ask children to send personal information to me.

If a child or parent contacts me by email, the parent or guardian should avoid including unnecessary information about the child.

✧

4. Local Notifications

If reminders are enabled, the Game may request notification permission and schedule local reminders for gameplay events such as hunger, daily rewards, a free heal, or competitions.

How Reminders Work

These reminders are generated and scheduled on the device. The Game does not currently use a developer-operated push-notification server or use notifications for behavioral advertising.

Managing Notifications

Notification permission can be:

  • Denied when permission is requested.
  • Disabled through the Game's settings.
  • Withdrawn through the device's notification settings.

Disabling notifications does not prevent the player from using the rest of the Game.

5. Device Permissions and Consent Choices

The Game requests permissions only when they are needed for a particular feature:

Permission When it is requested Purpose and method Effect of refusing or withdrawing it
Camera When the player opens the barcode scanner and chooses to allow access. The camera feed is processed locally to decode a barcode value. Images and video are not saved or uploaded by the Game. Barcode scanning will not work. The rest of the Game remains available.
Notifications When a parent or player enables Care Reminders. The operating system schedules optional local gameplay reminders. Reminders remain off or are cancelled. The rest of the Game remains available.

Permission Timing

The Game does not request these sensitive permissions repeatedly at first launch. Permission prompts appear in the relevant feature context.

Where consent is required by law, consent is requested before the relevant permission-protected information is processed.

Permission can be withdrawn at any time through the Game or device settings, as described on the Privacy Rights page.

◇

6. Backup Files and User-Directed Sharing

A parent or guardian can export a JSON backup containing local profiles and gameplay progress.

The backup may include the locally stored information described in this policy, including:

  • Nicknames
  • Birth years
  • Scanned barcode values
  • Hatchlings
  • Inventory
  • Virtual currency
  • Achievements
  • Game settings

How Backup Export Works

The Game temporarily writes the backup to app storage and then opens the device share sheet or Android folder picker.

The parent chooses the destination, which may include device storage, email, cloud storage, or another installed application.

Important: Backup Files Are Not Encrypted

Exported backup files are not encrypted by the Game.

Parents should keep exported backups private, share them only with trusted services, and delete copies that are no longer required.

Once a backup is sent to another application or cloud provider, that provider's privacy terms apply.

Importing a Backup

Importing a backup replaces or restores local gameplay information.

A local safety copy may be created during the import process to reduce the risk of accidental data loss.

7. In-App Purchases

The Game contains a parent-gated Bank for optional Sparkle packages. Availability depends on the installed version and storefront.

When Real-Money Purchases Are Enabled

  • Huawei AppGallery purchases may be processed by Huawei AppGallery / HMS Core In-App Purchases.
  • Purchases on supported storefronts may be processed through RevenueCat and the applicable platform store.
  • The store or purchase provider may process store-account information, an anonymous customer identifier, product and price information, transaction identifiers, purchase history, and device or technical information required for purchase processing, fraud prevention, verification, fulfilment, and restoration.

Payment Information

I do not receive or store a player's full payment-card number, bank-account details, or store password.

Those details are handled by the applicable store or payment provider.

Purchase Delivery Records

The Game keeps a limited local transaction-delivery ledger so that paid Sparkles are delivered exactly once and can be recovered if the Game closes during delivery.

This record is not included in ordinary gameplay backup restoration. This helps prevent the same purchase from being delivered twice.

Third-Party Purchase Providers

Purchases may involve third-party storefronts and purchase services. These providers process information under their own privacy policies and legal responsibilities.

Relevant provider policies include:

  • Huawei AppGallery Privacy Statement
  • HMS Core Privacy Information
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy , if purchases are provided through Google Play.
  • Apple App Store & Privacy , if an iOS version is provided.

8. Purpose, Scope, and Method of Processing

Why Information Is Used

Information is used only as reasonably necessary to:

  • Create and manage local profiles.
  • Save and restore gameplay progress.
  • Provide age-appropriate settings and parental controls.
  • Operate the scanner, notifications, backups, accessibility settings, and other requested features.
  • Process, verify, deliver, and restore optional purchases.
  • Protect purchases against duplicate delivery, fraud, or technical failure.
  • Maintain Game security and diagnose a problem reported by a player or parent.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

No Targeted Advertising

I do not use gameplay information to build advertising profiles or to show targeted advertising.

Scope and Method

The scope of processing is limited to the information and features described in this policy.

Local information is read and written through the Game's private device storage.

Camera and notification information is handled through operating-system permission APIs.

Purchase information is exchanged with the applicable store or purchase provider over their secure network interfaces when a parent initiates or restores a purchase.

I do not secretly collect information or reuse it for an unrelated purpose.

Data Minimisation

I apply data minimisation. The Game is designed to work without a Hatchlings & Heroes online account, email address, telephone number, contacts, precise location, microphone, advertising identifier, or public player profile.

9. Legal Bases

Depending on the player's country and the feature involved, I rely on one or more of the following legal bases:

Consent

For optional camera access, notifications, and other processing where consent is required.

A parent or guardian provides consent for a child where applicable law requires it.

Providing the Requested Game Service

To create a local profile, save progress, process a parent-initiated purchase, deliver purchased Sparkles, and restore eligible purchases.

Legitimate Interests

Where permitted, to protect transaction integrity, prevent duplicate purchase delivery, maintain security, and diagnose a problem.

These interests are balanced against the rights and interests of children and other players.

Legal Obligation

Where information must be processed or retained to comply with tax, accounting, consumer-protection, law-enforcement, or other applicable requirements.

Withdrawing Consent

If consent is withdrawn, I stop the consent-based processing affected by that withdrawal.

Withdrawal does not make earlier lawful processing invalid and does not prevent access to unrelated Game features.

I do not sell personal information. I do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising or targeted advertising.

When Information May Leave the Device

Information may leave the device only in limited circumstances:

11. Advertising Identifiers, Advertising, and Marketing

No Third-Party Advertising

The Game does not contain third-party advertising, personalized advertising, or marketing profiling.

It does not request or use Huawei's Open Advertising Identifier (OAID), Google's Advertising ID, or another advertising identifier for advertising or analytics.

No Advertising Identifier Profiling

The Game does not combine advertising identifiers with names, account information, or permanent device identifiers.

Because the Game does not use an advertising identifier, OAID collection, association, reset-linking, and personalized-advertising controls are not applicable to the Game's own processing.

App Store Services Are Separate

Huawei's separate AppGallery application may process information under Huawei's own privacy statement. That processing is separate from developer-operated Hatchlings & Heroes processing.

⌁

12. Retention and Deletion

Local Profile and Gameplay Information

Local profile and gameplay information remains on the device until a profile is deleted through the Game, the Game's storage is cleared through device settings, or the Game is uninstalled.

Individual profile deletion is protected by a parent gate.

Exported Backup Files

Exported backup files remain wherever the parent chose to save them until the parent deletes them.

Deleting a profile in the Game does not delete copies previously exported to Files, email, cloud storage, or another application.

Purchase Records

Local purchase-delivery records may be kept for as long as reasonably necessary to confirm delivery and prevent duplicate fulfilment.

Purchase records held by Huawei, RevenueCat, Google, Apple, or another store are retained according to that provider's policies and legal obligations.

No Remote Gameplay Account

Because ordinary gameplay information is stored locally rather than in a Hatchlings & Heroes online account, I normally cannot remotely view or delete that local information.

A parent can remove it directly using the controls described above.

✧

13. Security

I use reasonable security measures appropriate to a local mobile game. The level of protection depends on the type of information involved and the way the Game stores or processes it.

Local Security

The active profile identifier and parental-gate setting use the device's secure-storage service where supported. Most gameplay information is stored in the Game's private local app storage rather than on a developer-operated server.

Network Security

Network communication initiated by configured app-store and purchase providers is expected to use encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections provided by those services.

I do not send ordinary gameplay save data to a developer-operated server.

Important: No Storage Method Is Completely Secure

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Exported backup files should not be treated as encrypted or secret once they have been shared outside the Game. Parents and guardians are responsible for choosing a trusted backup destination and protecting access to the device, backup files, and relevant app-store account.

Protecting Your Device

  • Use a device lock, PIN, password, or supported biometric protection.
  • Keep the device and operating system reasonably up to date.
  • Only share exported Game backups with people or services you trust.
  • Delete backup copies that are no longer needed.
  • Protect the app-store account used for purchases.

14. International Processing

Hatchlings & Heroes is designed primarily around local processing on the player's device. However, some information may be processed outside the player's country when a third-party service is involved.

When International Processing May Occur

If a purchase, backup-sharing service, app store, or support email provider processes information outside the player's country, the information may be handled in countries that have different data-protection laws from those in the player's country.

Third-Party Transfer Safeguards

The relevant provider is responsible for the international transfer safeguards that apply to information it processes. These safeguards may include contractual, technical, organizational, or other measures required by applicable law.

Third-Party Services

Where information is processed directly by an app store, purchase provider, backup destination, or email provider, that provider's own privacy policy and international-transfer practices apply to its processing.

Because ordinary gameplay save data is stored locally and is not sent to a developer-operated gameplay server, routine gameplay does not normally involve international transfer of the Game's ordinary local save data by me.

15. Privacy Rights and Parent Requests

Depending on where the player lives, a parent or guardian may have rights to ask about, access, correct, restrict, object to, or delete a child's personal information.

Most Game Data Is Controlled on the Device

Most Game data can be viewed, copied, corrected, or deleted directly on the device because it is stored locally.

Detailed instructions for reviewing local information, exporting a copy, renaming or deleting a profile, withdrawing camera or notification permission, and contacting me are provided on the Privacy Rights page.

View the Privacy Rights Page →

Local Profiles, Not Online Accounts

The Game has local profiles rather than registered online accounts. There is therefore no remote Hatchlings & Heroes account to deregister.

Deleting a local profile removes its profile-scoped Game data from the device. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the Game removes the remaining local Game data, subject to the behavior of the operating system and app store.

Previously Exported Information

Deleting information inside the Game does not automatically delete copies that were previously exported or shared.

Previously exported backups, copies stored in cloud services, email attachments, or records held independently by a purchase provider must be deleted separately through the relevant destination or provider.

Information Held by App Stores or Purchase Providers

For information held directly by an app store or purchase provider, privacy requests should normally be made through that provider's own privacy controls or support channels.

Those providers may have their own procedures for accessing, correcting, restricting, or deleting information they hold.

Questions or Privacy Requests

For questions about Hatchlings & Heroes or information voluntarily sent to me, parents and guardians can contact me using the details provided below.

I may need reasonable information to verify that a requester is the child's parent or legal guardian before responding to a request concerning a child's personal information.

What a Privacy Request Should Include

  • The nature of the request.
  • Which Game profile or information the request concerns, where applicable.
  • Enough information for me to understand and respond to the request.
  • Reasonable evidence of parental or guardian authority where required.

Please do not send unnecessary sensitive information when making a privacy request.
