I respect your privacy and aim to collect only the information that is reasonably necessary to operate this website and respond to enquiries.


About This Website

This website is a personal professional portfolio belonging to:

Annuscha Botes
South Africa

The website showcases my work, including UX/UI design, web design, creative projects, game development, writing, visual design, and selected case studies.

This website is intended primarily as a professional portfolio and creative showcase.

If you use the contact form, you may voluntarily provide:

annuschabotes@gmail.com

Information collected through this website may be used to:

Technicalities


Cookies

This website may use cookies and similar technologies.

Cookies are small files stored on your device that can help a website remember information or understand how visitors interact with it.

Cookies may be used for purposes such as:

  • Website performance
  • Analytics
  • Improving user experience

Some cookies may be placed by third-party services used by the website.

You can usually control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

Analytics

If analytics tools are used on this website, they may collect information about how visitors interact with the website.

This may include information such as:

  • Pages viewed

Analytics information is used to understand website performance and improve the design and user experience.
