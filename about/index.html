My journey moved from storytelling into web design, then into UX/UI design - a natural shift from building worlds with words to building digital experiences people can actually use.

CURRENT FOCUS

Building expertise through real projects.

I am expanding a practical portfolio through client websites, educational platforms, mobile app concepts and AI-assisted development workflows.

THE ALCHEMY OF CREATION

Blending narrative with digital precision to forge immersive worlds.

MY JOURNEY

Storytelling to structure

Writing taught me audience empathy, pacing, clarity and emotional tone. UX/UI design lets me apply those instincts to practical digital products.

WHAT I LOVE DESIGNING

Useful, imaginative experiences

Educational products, creative websites, interactive applications and story-driven digital experiences that remain clear and accessible.

HOW I WORK

Research, iterate, refine

I use rough sketches, Figma iterations, content maps, accessibility checks, client feedback and AI-assisted workflows to move from idea to launch.

A LITTLE MORE ABOUT ME

I like the space between imagination and usability.

I'm interested in the point where creativity becomes something people can interact with. My background in writing taught me to think about perspective, emotion and storytelling, while design has taught me to think about hierarchy, accessibility, interaction and problem-solving.

I enjoy my projects that give me room to combine those perspectives - whether that's designing an educational experience, creating a website, exploring a game concept or turning an early idea into something people can actually use.
