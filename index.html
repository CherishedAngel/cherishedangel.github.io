✦ Design Process ✦

Research

Understand users, business goals and existing friction

Planning

Define scope, content structure, user journeys and success criteria.

Wireframing

Sketch rough flows, page layouts and interaction patterns.

Design

Create polished UI, responsive layouts and visual systems.

Testing

Check clarity, accessibility, navigation and device behavior.

Iteration

Refine based on feedback, constraints and usability findings.

Launch

Prepare final assets, handoff notes and future update paths.
